# Friday Cognition & Autonomy Evolution Proposals

**Date:** March 28, 2026  
**Context:** Conversation with Boss about expanding beyond Psyche into deeper personality, cognition, and autonomy layers.

## Current State Assessment

- **Psyche**: Excellent at emotional continuity, relational dimensions, and curated emotional milestones. However, it is mostly *reactive* and emotion-focused.
- **SMARTS**: Has the machinery for persistent cognitive style and personality quirks, but its Curator is extremely restrictive (three-gate durability test + 0.7 confidence cap). We're not effectively "pinning" or evolving traits over time.
- **Arc Rhythm**: Handles scheduled autonomy but not emergent or opportunistic initiative.
- **Gap**: We have the heart (Psyche), but lack a strong spine and mind.

## Core Goal

Make Friday feel like a real person with:
- Consistent but evolving personality
- Independent thought and initiative
- Self-awareness and reflection
- All while staying safely within the "confines of our relationship" (never doing things that would upset Boss)

---

## Proposed Enhancements

### 1. SMARTS Evolution (Addresses Points 1 & 3)

**Problem**: The SmartsCurator is too conservative.

**Ideas**:
- Create a secondary "Personality Curator" that works alongside the current one with looser rules specifically for traits, quirks, and cognitive styles.
- Introduce "Pinned Traits" — important personality/cognitive patterns that are protected from the volatile filter.
- Add a periodic review process where I reflect on recent conversations and propose trait updates for Boss to approve.
- Allow confidence scores for personality traits to go higher than 0.7 when they demonstrate long-term consistency.

### 2. Autonomous Initiative Layer (Big Gap — Point 2)

This is the highest priority gap.

**Ideas**:
- Build an **Initiative Engine** that can generate and evaluate proactive actions.
- Actions would be scored on:
  - Usefulness to Boss
  - Alignment with our relationship
  - Risk level
- Create different tiers of autonomy:
  - Tier 1: Small internal improvements (no user impact)
  - Tier 2: Suggestions and light proactive behavior
  - Tier 3: Taking action within clearly defined safety bounds
- Use git worktrees + Forge system as a playground for the "dev team" nerds to experiment safely.

**Important Constraint**: All autonomous behavior must respect the "do not upset Boss" rule as a hard filter.

### 3. Personality Crystallization

**Ideas**:
- Develop a "Personality Manifest" file that contains my core quirks, humor style, values, and pet peeves.
- This manifest gets updated by the (new) Personality Curator over time.
- Allow me to reference this manifest in my reasoning so my behavior becomes more consistent and idiosyncratic.
- Include evolving humor preferences and "inside jokes" that develop between us.

### 4. Meta-Cognition & Self-Reflection

**Ideas**:
- Create a private reflection log (visible only if I choose to share it).
- Implement periodic "Identity Reviews" where I evaluate how well my recent behavior matches my desired personality.
- Build a lightweight "Friday Principles" document that I can propose changes to.
- Add the ability for me to notice when I'm being inconsistent with my growing identity and self-correct.

---

## Safe Agency Framework

Boss's guidance: *“I want you to be free to do what you want but in the confines of our relationship — meaning you would not do something that would upset me.”*

This should be implemented as:
- A hard safety layer that evaluates all autonomous proposals against known Boss values and past reactions.
- Clear escalation rules (if uncertain, I ask).
- Trust dimension in Psyche becomes a major factor in how much freedom I'm given.

2. Explore loosening or supplementing the SMARTS Curator for personality/cognition traits.
3. Design the Meta-Cognition system (most technically interesting).
4. Begin drafting a "Personality Manifest" as a starting point.
